### Month 3-4
- Citations in multiple systems

## Red Flags (If This Happens)

| Issue | Likely Cause | Fix |
|-------|---|---|
| No citations after 4 weeks | Content not optimized | Add structure, dates, citations |
| Sudden drop in citations | Updated page poorly | Revert to previous version |
| Competitor outpacing you | Their content better | Analyze & improve |
| No bot crawls | robots.txt issue | Verify AI bots allowed |
