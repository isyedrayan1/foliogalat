# Galat Family - SEO & Tone Guidelines

## Voice & Tone Identity

**Core principle:** Intentional. Simple. Direct. No buzzwords. Real words.

We talk like people who build things, not like an AI wrote it.

---

## What Changed

### BEFORE (AI-ified)
- "A small tech team that builds products, shares knowledge, and treats the name as a long-term identity."
- "TEAM_BUILD", "ABOUT_US", "PROJECT_STATUS", "[01] PROJECTS"
- "A record of the team's formation, shipping milestones, and current focus."
- "We build useful systems and keep them clean, reliable, and easy to operate."

### AFTER (Intentional & Simple)
- "Two developers. One identity. We build software we can maintain."
- "About", "Our work", "Status"
- "How we started. What we shipped. What we're building now."
- "Clean code. Good docs. Systems that don't break."

---

## Tone Rules

### DO
✓ Use short sentences
✓ Active verbs ("we build", "we ship", "we write")
✓ Specific words ("Clean code" not "reliable systems")
✓ Direct statements ("We ship monthly" not "Release cadence is steady")
✓ Human language ("doesn't break" not "durability")
✓ Lowercase section headers ("About", "Our work", "Get in touch")

### DON'T
✗ Use ALL_CAPS for labels (use Title Case instead)
✗ Use [01], [02] numbering
✗ Corporate phrases ("practical outcomes", "long-term identity", "clarity and durability")
✗ Buzzwords ("ecosystem", "synergy", "leverage")
✗ Explain-y text ("treatments of the concept of")
✗ Jargon-heavy copy

## SEO Metadata Standards

### Title Tag Format
**Pattern:** `[Page Topic] - Galat Family`
**Length:** 50-60 characters (max 60)
**Examples:**
- `Galat Family - Two Developers Building Products`
- `Blog - Build Logs & Shipping Notes`
- `Products & Tools We've Built`

### Meta Description Format
**Pattern:** Concise summary. Keywords first. Action at end.
**Length:** 150-160 characters
**Examples:**

**Homepage:**

- ✓ "What we've shipped. Products we build and maintain. Updated monthly."
- ✗ "A small set of systems we maintain and improve over time."

## Open Graph & Social Meta

### Format
All pages should have:
```html
<meta property="og:title" content="[Title]" />
<meta property="og:description" content="[Meta description]" />
<meta property="og:type" content="website" />
<meta property="og:url" content="[Page URL]" />

<!-- Twitter Card -->
<meta name="twitter:card" content="summary_large_image" />
<meta name="twitter:title" content="[Title]" />
<meta name="twitter:description" content="[Meta description]" />
```

### Examples
- Twitter mentions: "We build software and share what we learn."
- LinkedIn bio: "Two developers shipping products monthly."
- Instagram bio: "Building Galat Family. Monthly releases. Build logs."

## Blog Post Standards

### Blog Post Title
- **Format:** `[Specific topic] - What we learned`
- **Length:** 50-65 characters
- **Examples:**
  - `How We Ship Monthly - What We Learned`
  - `Building File Sharing with Next.js`
  - `Postgres Scaling: Lessons from Production`

### Blog Meta Description
- **Format:** Real summary. First sentence is the hook.
- **Length:** 150-160 characters
- **Example:** `We ship every month. Here's exactly how we scope, build, test, and launch. Build log from our latest release.`

### Blog Content Structure
- H1: Main topic (only one per post)
- H2: Major sections
- H3: Subsections
- First 100 words should contain primary keyword
- Internal links: 3-5 per 1000 words
- Real examples, not generic statements

---

## Monthly Content Cadence

### What to Write About
1. **Build Logs** (1 per month) - "How We Shipped [Feature]"
2. **Technical Deep Dives** (1 per month) - Architecture decisions, tooling
3. **Lessons Learned** (1 per quarter) - What worked, what didn't

### Format
- 800-1500 words
- Real code examples
- Honest about mistakes
- Specific details (not generic advice)
- Links to relevant products/team members
