## The Strategy: 3 Pillars

### Pillar 1: Structure
Make your content extractable for AI systems:
- 40-60 word answer blocks (optimal for snippets)
- Comparison tables (instead of prose)
- Clear definitions in first paragraph
- FAQ sections with Q&A pairs
- Numbered lists for processes
- Statistics with sources

**Result:** AI can understand and cite your content clearly

### Pillar 2: Authority
Make your content citable (trustworthy):
- Add statistics with sources: +37% AI visibility
- Add citations/references: +40% AI visibility
- Add expert quotes: +30% AI visibility
- Expert attribution (your team by name)
- Freshness signals ("Last updated: date")
- Transparent sourcing

**Result:** AI systems prefer to cite you over competitors

### Pillar 3: Presence
Be where AI looks:
- Allow AI bots (done ✓)
- Provide context files (done ✓)
- Make data machine-readable (done ✓)
- Create original research
- Build Wikipedia presence (if applicable)
- Participate in Reddit authentically
- Get on review sites

**Result:** AI systems actively find and recommend you

## Avoiding Common Mistakes

**DON'T DO THIS:**
- ✗ Write separate content "for AI" (risks spam policy)
- ✗ Keyword stuff (loses 10% AI visibility)
- ✗ Chunk content into tiny fragments
- ✗ Gate your best content (AI can't access it)
- ✗ Block AI bots in robots.txt
- ✗ Generate content at scale for ranking manipulation

**DO THIS INSTEAD:**
- ✓ Write for humans first, optimize for AI second
- ✓ Use natural paragraph + heading structure
- ✓ Keep content self-contained and extractable
- ✓ Show your best content publicly
- ✓ Create original, authentic content
- ✓ Allow AI bots (already done ✓)
